## Fix
`tests/unit/sidebar-ux-css.test.ts` now checks the V2 contract instead of pre-V2 utility strings:
- The owning control is the top-level `.accordion-header` button, and the toggle is a decorative span. Its ≥44px minimum height comes from `sidebar-categories.css`.
- The nested toggle has a ≥44×44 minimum (`sidebar-tree.css`).
- Leaf links ellipsise on purpose and carry their full name in `title`.
- The mobile cutoff is the exclusive `max-width: 767px` (`ui/sidebar.tsx`). 768–1024px keeps the tablet sidebar visible.

**Real defect found by the browser run.** The keyboard focus ring on top-level `.accordion-header` was clipped by the parent's `overflow` at every width, so it was invisible at the left and right edges.
- Fix: `client/src/styles/shell/sidebar-categories.css` adds a `:focus-visible` rule with `outline-offset: -2px`. This is an app-owned override; no frozen file was touched.
- A unit assertion locks the rule in.
