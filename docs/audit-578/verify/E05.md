# E05: Digest queue health stayed stale after the first load

**Status: FIXED, proven on DEV (2026-10-09)**

## Root cause (confirmed)
`DigestQueueHealth` used `useQuery` with only a queryKey. The global defaults (infinite staleTime, no interval, no
focus refetch) meant it never refreshed after a successful load, and the Retry button only existed in the error state.

## Fix (`client/src/components/admin/DigestQueueHealth.tsx`)
- Polls every 30 s with a finite staleTime (15 s), refetches on focus, `retry:false`.
- An "Updated hh:mm:ss · refreshes every 30s" label, plus a **Refresh** button available in every state.
- When a poll fails after a good load, the last snapshot stays on screen with a "Couldn't refresh health data …
  may be out of date" alert, and the label switches to "Last good update …".
- Related: a failed stats poll no longer blanks the whole admin dashboard (see E04). Before that change, the
  automatic-poll failure below replaced the console with "Error loading admin dashboard" (observed at t+30 s in the
  first outage run).

## Before / after
Before: the queue total never changed until a full reload, and there was no Refresh while healthy. After: the total
updates automatically within 30 s or immediately via Refresh, and a failed poll is shown as stale.
