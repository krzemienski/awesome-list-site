## Defect found and fixed: reorder focus

**Before**, in the first desktop run against unmodified code:
- After step-down, `document.activeElement` was the `steps-dialog` container, and Tab went to `add-step-button` at the top of the dialog.
- After step-up, focus was also on the container.

Cause: the arrows are `disabled` while the reorder request is in flight, and React moves the card's DOM node. Both of these blur the focused button.

**Fix** (`client/src/components/admin/JourneyStepsManager.tsx`):
- `move()` records the moved group and the direction.
- The reorder mutation stays pending until the steps list has been refetched.
- An effect then focuses the moved card's same-direction arrow. At a list boundary, where that arrow is disabled, it focuses the opposite arrow.

**After**, at both 1440 and 402:
- After step-down, focus is on `step-up-<triple>`, because down is now disabled at the bottom. Tab then goes to the triple's first resource link.
- After step-up, focus is on `step-down-<triple>`, because up is disabled at the top.

Not changed: after a confirmed delete or remove-resource, focus lands on the steps dialog container. The trigger button no longer exists, and focus stays inside the modal, so the next Tab enters the list. This is acceptable "surviving focus" and was not changed.
