## Residuals (measured, diagnosed, NOT passing)
1. **category / subcategory in Terminal, Geist, Brutalist and Swiss (3.1–6.0% at 1440, 5.6–8.7% at 375).** After the chip fix, the crops match glyph for glyph. The remaining diff is a 1px vertical offset that starts inside the second card row and accumulates with each of the 8 card rows (row 1 titles land at the same y; in row 2 a title is at y 476 in the reference and 477 in the app, and the offset keeps growing). Every text line below that point then fails pixelmatch.
   - The cause is sub-pixel rounding of fractional card heights. Non-Editorial chip and leading metrics give 26.797px chips and 20.15px lines, and the reference and app round them at different boundaries.
   - Editorial's metrics happen to round identically, which is why the control passes.
   - Not fixed: matching it would mean hard-coding per-system pixel heights onto content-sized cards.
2. **Admin rows (0.58–4.24%).** These pass in Editorial (control 24/24). The diff is the same 1px offset, starting at the table card heading ("Resources (25 of 1,816)", y≈334 in the Terminal resources diff), after which every row's text shifts by 1px. The "View" ghost button ink is also brighter in the app; that is the I01 accessible-ink deviation and is intentional.
3. **Submit at 375 (Terminal 0.77, Brutalist 0.75): approved deviation.** The reference's actions row is 256px wide inside a 237px content box and overflows into the card padding (its Cancel starts at x=49 inside a 28px-padded card). The app's buttons carry `min-w-[44px]`, so they flex-shrink into the box (probe: 75.5/151.5px wide at 375, 80.8/166.6 at 1440, padding 9px 16px on both sides). The app keeps the actions inside the box, and the label is not clipped (scrollWidth 150 ≤ 151.5). This is not lowered to the reference's overflow.
4. **Accessible-ink deviations (I01/I02)** are explicit by design: `--accent-ink` on small accent text and black `--on-accent` labels on filled primary buttons. Contrast is never lowered to match pixels.
