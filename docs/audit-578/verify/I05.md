## Residuals (as measured in this task; see #611 for closure)
1. **category / subcategory in Terminal, Geist, Brutalist and Swiss: RESOLVED in #611.** The "1px offset per card row" was not sub-pixel rounding. Every row now passes at all four widths (table below).
2. **Admin rows: RESOLVED in #611** except the documented deviations below. The same hard-coded leading caused the offset from the table-card heading; the rest came from per-system skin overrides and reference-adapter cascade artefacts (see #611 fixes).
3. **Submit at 375 (Terminal 0.77, Brutalist 0.75): approved deviation.** The reference's actions row is 256px wide inside a 237px content box and overflows into the card padding (its Cancel starts at x=49 inside a 28px-padded card). The app keeps the actions inside the box, and the label is not clipped (scrollWidth 150 ≤ 151.5). This is not lowered to the reference's overflow.
4. **Accessible-ink deviations (I01/I02)** are explicit by design: `--accent-ink` on small accent text and black `--on-accent` labels on filled primary buttons. Contrast is never lowered to match pixels.
