## Fix: completion heading

`client/src/pages/JourneyDetail.tsx` used `<h3>🎉 Congratulations!</h3>`. The emoji showed as a missing-glyph box wherever no colour-emoji font is installed (screenshot `shots-592/V05-completed-desktop-BEFORE-emoji-fix.png`: "▯ Congratulations!"), and screen readers announced "party popper". The trophy icon already decorates the card, so the emoji was removed. After: `shots-592/V05-completed-desktop.png` shows "Congratulations!" under the trophy (re-run after the fix: same 18/15 results, residue 0).
