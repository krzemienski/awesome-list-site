# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing and resource discovery pages: Home, categories and taxonomy listings, tag landings, resources, learning journeys, About, Advanced, Submit, and public collection shares.
- Public legal/community policy pages (`/terms`, `/privacy`, `/code-of-conduct`).
- Search/social previews, AI crawler visibility, and source-detectable WCAG 2.2 A/AA accessibility on public pages and shared chrome.

## Out of scope
- Authenticated dashboards, profile, bookmarks, recommendations, notifications and internal tools.
- Admin routes (`/admin/**`).
- Account and preference/settings utilities except their search exclusion contract.
- APIs except where they directly determine public delivery, content visibility, or crawl files.
- Design-system previews and historical evidence artifacts are not public search landing pages.

## Target audience
- Developers, engineers, and technical learners looking for video development resources.

## Primary keywords
- awesome video resources
- video development resources
- video streaming tools
- video codecs
- video players
- FFmpeg resources

## Content and rendering strategy
- The curated video-development index and guided learning journeys are the principal discoverable content; provenance, legal policies and contribution information support trust.
- React/Vite frontend with Express delivery. Anonymous production Home GET requests (supported layout parameters and no session cookie) receive exact-tree React SSR through `server/ssr.ts` and `client/src/entry-server.tsx`.
- Other public routes use SPA hydration plus initial semantic HTML and route-specific metadata/JSON-LD supplied by `server/og-middleware.ts` and `server/seo-content.ts`. Do not treat them as empty SPAs without inspecting the injector.
- Home/Advanced headings are shared through `shared/page-headings.ts`. Legal routes render shared `LegalPolicyContent` on both passes, including cookie-table header associations on mobile.
- The About semantic body currently includes biography and FAQs but omits additional public project sections; About/Submit primary headings also differ between server and client.
- Sitemap is generated by `server/routes/non-api.ts`; public route status and noindex handling are resolved before static fallback.
- Server canonical origin is `PUBLIC_SITE_URL` or `https://awesome.video`. `vite.config.ts` defines the matching client origin and intentionally ignores the retired legacy `VITE_SITE_URL` value.
- Preserve source-visible route-specific metadata, stable internal links and canonical-only indexable sitemap entries. Utility/private screens should remain excluded from search.

## GEO and AI crawlers
- AI citation of public resource discovery content is in scope.
- `client/public/robots.txt` explicitly allows named AI search/assistant crawlers. Private-route exclusions are repeated in wildcard, social, search-engine, and AI groups; named groups do not inherit wildcard exclusions.
- `client/public/llms.txt` describes authoritative public content and citation patterns.
- About FAQ content is shared between JSON-LD and both body render passes.
- Primary deployment is on Replit; checked-in Cloudflare Container deployment serves `cf.awesome.video` while retaining apex canonicals. Cloudflare edge AI-bot settings are not established by source and must align with the intended public AI access; no live blocking has been confirmed.

## Brand and performance assumptions
- Custom AV monogram favicon SVG/ICO, Apple touch icon, manifest and PNG app icons are present under `client/public` and referenced by the shell.
- Google Fonts use preconnect and print-media async loading with swap; category glyph fonts are small local files. Route lazy loading and explicit vendor chunks are configured. No source-proven >2 MB crawler-critical resource was established in this scan; this is not a measured performance claim.
