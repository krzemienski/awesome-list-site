## Exclusions and reasons

- Individual production categories, tags, resources, journeys and collections are represented by their page types and control classes, not duplicated thousands of times; dynamic values still require evidence per sampled instance.
- Design-system and Theme preview controls explicitly marked `inert`/display-only are not counted as working interactions; their inertness itself is covered by C6-PS-044.
- `awesome-list-site-ds/` and `awesome-list-site-ds-20260929/` are frozen design references, not runtime routes; runtime `client/src` is the inventory source.
- Admin-only screens are outside this public/member file. The separately produced full inventory includes them, but this report deliberately covers only `ux-public-core`, `ux-public-secondary`, `ux-a11y`, and `ux-member` as requested.
- Third-party Clerk widget internals (OAuth, email verification, CAPTCHA and account portal) are bounded to app route, redirect, loading and unavailable states because a real member session was out of reach in cycle 5. C6-MB-079 keeps that as an explicit verification requirement rather than assuming those flows.
- Production-destructive actions are inventoried but restricted to cancel/confirmation inspection unless the audit’s production-write rules permit creation and cleanup of a new QA row. Paid launches, real user deletion, bulk approvals and owner sign-out-all are never to be executed for coverage.
